CanSat Satellite Axis Reference

In aerospace physics, the onboard 3-axis accelerometer and gyroscope measure real-time rotation and gravitational decomposition:

Z-Axis (Yaw)

Central vertical axis. Rotating around Z spins the CanSat in place upright (like twisting the antenna line).

X-Axis (Roll)

Horizontal lateral axis. Tilting side-to-side changes the X-axis acceleration component.

Y-Axis (Pitch)

Horizontal axis. Tilting the satellite forward or backward alters the Y-axis acceleration component.

Acceleration Equations (m/s²):

Ax = -g · sin(Roll) · cos(Pitch)
Ay = g · sin(Pitch)
Az = g · cos(Roll) · cos(Pitch)   (g = 9.81 m/s²)
